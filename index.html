Educational Philosophy

"Mathematics and physics are tools for questioning, understanding, and shaping the world."

I believe mathematics and physics are not rigid lists of formulas to memorize, but powerful tools for questioning, understanding, and shaping the world around us. My classroom prioritizes active inquiry, psychological safety for mistake-driven growth, and differentiated support to build lasting confidence in every learner.
